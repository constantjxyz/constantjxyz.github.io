Yuzhang Xie

I am a Ph.D. student in Computer Science and Informatics at Emory University (2023 cohort), advised by Dr. Carl Yang and co-advised by Dr. Xiao Hu.

My research focuses on AI for health and biological and clinical informatics, with interests in knowledge graph-guided learning, graph learning, machine learning, and data mining. I work on methods that connect biomedical knowledge and clinical data to support reliable analysis and decision-making.

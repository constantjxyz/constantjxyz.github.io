Yuzhang Xie

I am a 4th-year Ph.D. candidate in Department of Computer Science at Emory University, advised by Dr. Carl Yang and co-advised by Dr. Xiao Hu. Before joining Emory, I graduated from Shanghai Jiao Tong University, where I was advised by Dr. Yang Yang.

I am currently seeking an internship. My work focuses on developing reliable and interpretable AI for healthcare by combining multimodal clinical data, language models, and biomedical knowledge.

  • Multimodal AI for Healthcare: Developing robust and interpretable AI methods that integrate diverse clinical data modalities, including tables, text, images, omics, and time-series data, to support clinical decision-making.
  • LLM Agents & Reliable Learning: Studying how LLM-based agents improve through interaction, fine-tuning, and feedback, and how to evaluate their reasoning and decisions reliably.
  • KG-Guided Learning & Reasoning: Integrating biomedical knowledge graphs into machine learning, especially LLMs, to support grounded reasoning, inference, and interpretability.
  • Data Mining & Representation Learning: Extracting knowledge from heterogeneous data and learning transferable representations with language models and graph neural networks.
